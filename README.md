## 🔬 Research

**Terrain-Masked Risk-Aware Path Planning for UAVs** · [`Path-Planing`](https://github.com/fandiyudha/Path-Planing)
Benchmarks A\*, Artificial Potential Field, and a Hybrid A\*–APF planner for nap-of-the-earth UAV flight over synthetic terrain with line-of-sight-masked radar threats. Fully reproducible Monte-Carlo framework (pure NumPy/SciPy), pre-registered evaluation protocol, Wilcoxon/Holm statistics, and a Streamlit dashboard. Submitted to *Defence Science Journal*.

**Quantum Decryption Risk to National Cryptographic Infrastructure**
Proposed the Quantum-Adjusted Risk Score (QARS), an extension of Mosca's Inequality, and applied it to Indonesian national digital identity and health systems. Presented at FET'26, Bahrain.

**Backdoor Detectability in Imported Intrusion Detection Models** · [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23295705](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23295705)
Shows that class properties explain why backdoors in imported ML-based IDS models are (or aren't) caught. Feature-space BadNets attacks on MLP and 1D-CNN detectors (CICIDS2017, UNSW-NB15), evaluated against Neural Cleanse, MM-BD, STRIP, and IBD-PSC, plus a model-intake screening-gate prototype. 134 released checkpoints. Manuscript under review.

**PQC Transition Forensics for UAV Infrastructure**
Network forensics and threat modelling of the ML-KEM post-quantum transition in SITL-based UAV infrastructure (co-author).

Commodity value-chain mapping platform for national research use. Industry trees for 28 priority commodities (648 products, 1,000+ links), trade and price forecasting, patent analytics over 100k+ records, and a local-LLM AI consultant via Ollama. PHP 8.2 · MySQL 8 · React 19 · TypeScript.
